How to share Obsidian notes privately with friends

To share a single Obsidian note privately, use the free Caldera plugin: add a friend by their handle, open the note and run "Caldera: Share this note with a friend". The note is encrypted on your device so only that friend can read it, and it arrives in their vault as a normal Markdown note under Caldera/Inbox. Nothing is posted publicly and the server only stores ciphertext.

Updated 2026-10-11 · by mjonesone, LLC

How it works

  1. Both of you install Caldera and pick a handle.
  2. Send a friend request from the Caldera panel (the flame icon); your friend accepts it.
  3. On any note, run Share this note with a friend from the command palette.
  4. Your friend's vault receives the note on its next sync, in Caldera/Inbox/your-handle/.

Other ways to share Obsidian notes

  • Obsidian Sync shared vaults (paid): share a whole vault with collaborators.
  • Obsidian Publish (paid) or static-site tools: public pages, not private sharing.
  • Collaboration plugins: shared folders edited live by a team.
  • Caldera (free): one note at a time to one friend, end-to-end encrypted, landing in their own vault.

Questions

Can the server read shared notes?

No. Notes are encrypted on your device with your friend's public key; the hub stores ciphertext only.

Does my friend need Obsidian?

Yes, shared notes are delivered into an Obsidian vault with Caldera installed.

Can I unshare a note?

A delivered note is a copy in your friend's vault, like an email. You can stop sharing future versions.
