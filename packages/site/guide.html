Change your page

New to Obsidian? Get it free at obsidian.md, or let the install prompt do it for you. Your page is built from your vault, so you change it the way you change notes. Pick whichever is easiest; they all end in the same place.

1Ask your AIPaste one prompt and describe the change. 2Edit a noteCaldera/profile.md holds every public field. 3Use commandsSettings and the command palette in Obsidian.

1. Ask your AI

Copy this into Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or any agent that can edit files in your vault. Replace the last line with what you want, like "make my headline about my new studio and add my Substack link". It shows you the change before it makes it.

caldera-change.md
  • Finds the right file
  • Shows you the change first
  • Keeps private things off your page
  • Never touches your key
Help me change my Caldera profile (https://caldera.social/@<my handle>/). Caldera builds that public page from my Obsidian vault.

Vault: <ABSOLUTE PATH TO MY OBSIDIAN VAULT>
(If I did not fill this in, find my vault yourself: it is the folder that contains a .obsidian folder. If there is more than one, ask me which.)

Where things live:
- Name, headline, photo, banner, links, properties, topics, which sections show, and directory listing: the frontmatter of "Caldera/profile.md". Keep its format. Photo and banner are paths to images inside the vault.
- A note on my page: add "caldera: public" to that note's frontmatter (title and first paragraph are shown). Remove it to take the note down.
- My daily journal: caldera_headline / caldera_focus / caldera_wins / caldera_themes in daily notes named YYYY-MM-DD.md.
- Activity counts: "Caldera/activity.json".
The page republishes automatically about a minute after Caldera/profile.md changes. For anything else, tell me to run "Caldera: Publish my profile".

Rules:
- Show me the exact change before you make it, and only make what I approve.
Everything public is on the open internet, can be copied forever, and can affect other people. Be very cautious. Never write any of these into anything public:
- Other people: no names, initials, handles, photos or descriptions that identify anyone I meet, work with, hire, date, advise or invest with. Say "a founder", "the team", "a friend" only when it adds nothing identifying - usually leave it out.
- Meetings: no meetings, calls, lunches, dinners, interviews or events - not their titles, times, dates, attendees, or that they happened with someone specific.
- Times and places: no times of day, schedules, travel, cities, venues, addresses, offices, home or "where I am".
- Secret or sensitive work: no unannounced, stealth, confidential or NDA projects, codenames, deals, fundraising, investors, acquisitions, valuations, hiring, firing or anything said in confidence.
- Things that can get someone in trouble: legal, medical, health, HR, money, salaries, debts, relationships, family, children, politics, religion.
- Contact details and links: no emails, phone numbers or links to private documents. No money amounts at all.
Describe work only by its public theme ("shipped a release", "wrote about design") and only name things I have already made public on my page (my own public projects, topics and links). If you are unsure, leave it out - an empty field is fine. Caldera also drops any journal line that looks sensitive before publishing.
- Never read or edit .obsidian/plugins/caldera/data.json - it holds my private key. My handle can only be changed in Settings -> Caldera.

What I want to change: <DESCRIBE THE CHANGE>

In Obsidian, Caldera: Copy AI prompt: change my profile gives you this prompt with your vault path and handle filled in.

2. Edit Caldera/profile.md

Caldera creates this note when you set up. Everything in its properties is public. Save it and your page republishes about a minute later. Run Caldera: Edit my profile to open it.

PropertyWhat it doesExample
name, headlineThe top of your pageheadline: "Designer. Writing about tools."
photo, bannerImages from your vaultphoto: "Attachments/me.jpg"
linksYour links, one per line- "Substack | https://you.substack.com"
propertiesShort facts- "Based in | Lisbon"
topicsWhich tags may be counted publicly- "design | Design"
sectionsSwitch parts of the page on or offjournal: false
listedShow your card on the People pagelisted: true

Notes and journal come from the rest of your vault:

  • caldera: public in a note's properties lists it on your page (title and first paragraph). Remove it to take it down.
  • caldera: friends marks a note you can share with friends as an encrypted card.
  • Daily notes named YYYY-MM-DD can carry caldera_headline, caldera_focus, caldera_wins, caldera_themes for your journal.
  • Caldera/activity.json adds daily counts (meetings, emails sent, AI prompts, commits, documents) to your score, plus optional blocks your AI agent can fill: ai (tokens by model and provider, for the AI usage charts), code (GitHub commits per week), knows and inbox. Counts only.

3. Commands

Open the command palette (Cmd/Ctrl+P) and type Caldera.

Open my public profileOpens your page in the browser
Copy AI prompt: change my profileThe prompt above, filled in for you

Copy AI agent setup promptThe daily journal and activity routine
Open friends panelFriends, requests, gravity (or click the flame icon)
Share this note with a friendSends an encrypted copy to a friend's vault
Post an ask / Post an offerThe help exchange
What's new in CalderaRelease notes
Delete my profile from the hubErases your page and everything tied to it

Your handle, hub and identity are in Settings → Caldera.

Your key, other devices, and deleting

  • There's no password. Your login is a key Caldera keeps in this vault, and the hub only accepts changes signed with it, so nobody else can edit your page.
  • Save it: Settings → Caldera → Export recovery key, into your password manager. Never paste it into an AI chat.
  • On a new device, open the same vault (Obsidian Sync, iCloud, git) and your key comes with it.
  • To leave, run Caldera: Delete my profile from the hub. Your vault isn't touched.
