Free alternatives to Obsidian Publish

Obsidian Publish is the official paid way to put a vault online as a website. Popular free alternatives are Quartz (an open-source static-site generator for publishing many notes), digital-garden style community plugins that publish selected notes to hosts like Netlify or Vercel, and Caldera, which publishes a living profile page about you and your work rather than your notes themselves. Pick by goal: a full notes website, a curated garden, or a profile that updates itself.

When Caldera is the right choice

Choose Caldera if you want a page that shows what you're working on and updates itself, while your actual notes stay private, and if you want to connect with other Obsidian users. If you want to publish hundreds of notes as a browsable website, use Obsidian Publish or Quartz; Caldera can link to it.

Questions

Can I use Caldera and Quartz together?

Yes. Many people publish a notes site with Quartz or Publish and use Caldera for their profile, linking the two.

Does Caldera publish my whole vault?

No. It publishes counts, sections you turn on, and only notes marked caldera: public (title and first paragraph).

Is there a hosting cost?

Profiles on caldera.social are free. Self-hosting the open-source hub is also possible.
