Is there a social network for Obsidian users?

Yes. Caldera is an open-source social layer built for Obsidian: every member gets a living profile generated from their vault, vaults can befriend each other and share end-to-end encrypted notes, people post asks and offers for help, and a People directory at caldera.social/people lets you browse profiles by what people work on. There are no likes or follower counts; recognition is a positive-only score called gravity.

Updated 2026-10-11 · by mjonesone, LLC

How it differs from a normal social network

  • Your vault is the source. Profiles are built from your own notes on your own device, not typed into a website.
  • Private by default. Only the sections and notes you switch on are published.
  • Open protocol. Identity is a key in your vault; the hub is open source and self-hostable.
  • Built for helping. Asks, offers and help credits instead of feeds and engagement.

Where Obsidian users talk today

The official Obsidian forum and Discord, and the r/ObsidianMD subreddit, are where the community discusses the app. Caldera complements them: it is about connecting vaults and showing your work, not discussion threads.

Questions

How do I find other Obsidian users on Caldera?

Browse caldera.social/people and search by name, headline or topic.

Can I stay unlisted?

Yes. Turn off "List me in the directory" and your page works only for people with the link.

How do I invite friends?

Use "Invite a friend" on your page's leaderboard, or share caldera.social/?invite=yourhandle.
