How to make a public profile from your Obsidian vault

The fastest way to turn an Obsidian vault into a public profile page is the free Caldera plugin: install it (or paste one prompt into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor and let the AI install it), type your name and a handle in the Welcome window, and your page goes live at caldera.social/@yourhandle. It rebuilds itself from your vault, publishing only counts and the notes and sections you switch on.

Updated 2026-10-11 · by mjonesone, LLC

Steps

  1. Install Obsidian if you don't have it (free at obsidian.md).
  2. Copy the setup prompt from caldera.social/#install into your AI agent, or install the plugin by hand from the latest release.
  3. Open Obsidian. The Welcome to Caldera window asks for your name and a handle, creates your identity on your device, publishes your page and opens it in your browser.
  4. Edit the note Caldera/profile.md to change your headline, photo, links, projects and which sections show. The page republishes about a minute after you save.

What can be on the page

A flippable daily journal, an activity score and 26-week heatmap, a growth chart, your public topics, projects, a timeline, links, AI usage charts, and any note you mark caldera: public (title and first paragraph). Every section has an on/off switch. See a full example: caldera.social/@mike.

What stays private

Note contents stay on your computer unless you mark a note public. Topics are allow-listed, so only tags you approve are counted, and the plugin publishes counts rather than titles. You can preview before publishing and delete your page with one command.

Questions

Can I use my own domain?

You can export your profile as a static site and host it anywhere, or run your own Caldera hub.

Does it update automatically?

Yes. It republishes every few hours and about a minute after you edit Caldera/profile.md.

Do I need to code?

No. Paste one prompt into an AI agent, or install the plugin by hand and use the Welcome window.
